Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Regalium may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit the Regalium website or interact with its publicly available services.

Regalium is designed around wallet-based blockchain interaction. A blockchain wallet address is publicly visible when you transact on-chain and should not be assumed to be anonymous.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may process information such as your wallet address when you connect a wallet, blockchain transaction data associated with your public address, information you voluntarily submit through contact or community channels, and technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network information, referring pages, and basic website usage data.

2. Blockchain Information

Blockchain networks are public. When you send or receive RGLM or interact with a Regalium smart contract, transaction information may be permanently recorded on the relevant blockchain and may be visible to anyone. Regalium cannot generally delete or alter public blockchain records.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and improve the website, provide requested services, facilitate wallet and blockchain interactions, communicate with users who contact us, monitor security and abuse, troubleshoot technical issues, understand website performance, comply with applicable legal obligations, and protect the rights and safety of the project and its users.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use essential browser storage, cookies, analytics, or similar technologies where implemented to maintain functionality, understand usage, improve performance, or protect the service. You can control certain browser storage and cookie behavior through your browser settings. Disabling required technologies may affect functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may link to or use third-party services such as cryptocurrency wallets, blockchain explorers, hosting providers, analytics services, community platforms, and infrastructure providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. Regalium is not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

6. Information Sharing

Regalium does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers who help operate the website, where necessary to comply with law or legal process, to protect users or the project, to investigate suspected abuse or fraud, or in connection with a business reorganization or transfer where legally permitted.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information under Regalium’s control. However, no website, network, wallet, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Regalium may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including security, legal, accounting, dispute-resolution, and operational requirements. Public blockchain records may remain available indefinitely because they are maintained by decentralized networks.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of certain information. These rights are subject to applicable legal limitations, including information that cannot be deleted from a public blockchain.

10. Children

The Regalium website is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to participate in digital-asset activities in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

11. International Users

Regalium may operate using service providers or infrastructure located in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for applicable international transfers.

12. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the website after an update indicates your acknowledgment of the revised Policy where permitted by law.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official Regalium contact channels published on the website. Do not send private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or other wallet credentials in a privacy request.

This Privacy Policy is a general template and should be reviewed and adapted by qualified legal counsel for Regalium’s operating entity, jurisdictions, analytics providers, and actual data practices.
